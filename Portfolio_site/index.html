About

I help organizations understand their security risks and prove their controls work. I train as a Cybersecurity GRC Analyst in the NTI Hire Ready Program, working with ISO/IEC 27001, PCI DSS, SOC 2, the CBE Cybersecurity Framework and SWIFT CSCF — and I back it with a real technical foundation in network security, firewalls and SOC practice.

My technical base comes from a Postgraduate Diploma in Cybersecurity (IT Gate Academy) — ~230 hands-on hours across network & OS administration, FortiGate firewalls (NSE 4), ethical hacking (CEH), Windows Server and SOC fundamentals — so I understand the controls GRC teams review, not just describe them. Three years of teaching at BHI and AASTMT taught me to explain technical topics simply, write clear documentation and give structured feedback.
